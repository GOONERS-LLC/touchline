# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-12

- Full time is now three screens. First the scoreboard with Match Facts, Ratings and Stats tabs along the top (goals mixed in order with cards, injuries and substitutions; both line-ups on the pitch with match ratings). Then Player of the Match on its own screen, tap them to view and make an offer. Then every result in your division that week.
- Formation boards are drawn from the real lines of each shape, so 4-3-3 reads as four, three, three. Eight new formations, 26 in total, each with a description of how it plays.
- Much richer commentary: fouls, corners, offsides, blocked shots, saves, misses, the woodwork, bookings and injuries all have their own descriptions.
- A live crowd: the home fans murmur, roar, groan, boo, applaud, gasp and chant in response to the match.
- Haptics throughout, with a setting to turn them off.
- Live Match Accessibility settings: commentary detail level, wait for VoiceOver to finish each line before the clock moves on, read the score after goals, quarter-hour updates, and a new Very Slow replay speed.
- Graphical kits. Clubs wear their real colours and famous patterns, shirt sponsors appear on the shirt, and the Kit Designer lets you change the pattern, colours, collar and sleeves. Supporters react to the new look, which moves merchandise income.
- Real 2026-27 squads from Transfermarkt for every league, including the Championship, League One and League Two: real names, ages, nationalities, positions and preferred feet.
- A decluttered home screen.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-12

First release of Touchline by Gooners LLC.

- New name, new identity and a full visual redesign using the Floodlight palette (navy, cobalt and amber), new main menu, home screen, match day screen, button styles and app icon.
- Continue to Match Day now returns after every match. Previously the calendar jumped straight onto the next fixture's date, so the home screen only ever offered Play.
- VoiceOver reads full club names everywhere; three-letter abbreviations are never spoken. Competition pickers use full competition names.
- Removed all emoji and symbol characters from the interface and documentation.
